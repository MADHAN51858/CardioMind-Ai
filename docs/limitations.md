# Limitations & Medical Safety Disclaimer

## This System Is NOT

- ❌ A medical diagnostic device
- ❌ A replacement for qualified healthcare professionals
- ❌ Clinically validated or regulatory-approved
- ❌ Suitable for emergency triage or treatment decisions
- ❌ An authority on individual patient outcomes

## This System IS

- ✅ An academic engineering prototype
- ✅ A demonstration of ML pipeline methodology
- ✅ A teaching tool for explainable AI concepts
- ✅ A research exercise in probability calibration
- ✅ A final-year project deliverable

## Dataset Limitations

1. **Sample Size (N=303)**: Far too small to represent global cardiovascular diversity. Results should not be generalized beyond the study population.
2. **Selection Bias**: All patients were referred for diagnostic coronary angiography at Cleveland Clinic Foundation, skewing the sample toward symptomatic individuals.
3. **Demographic Imbalance**: The dataset contains a disproportionately high number of male subjects and older age groups.
4. **Temporal Gap**: Data was collected circa 1988. Clinical standards, diagnostic criteria, treatment protocols, and population health profiles have all evolved significantly.
5. **Feature Scope**: Only 13 clinical variables are used. Important modern predictors (troponin levels, calcium scoring, genetic markers, lifestyle factors, medication history) are absent.
6. **Missing Data**: Columns `ca` and `thal` contain missing values that are imputed — any imputation introduces assumptions.

## Model Output Limitations

- Raw ML probabilities represent **decision boundary distances**, not calibrated clinical probabilities
- Even after Platt scaling calibration, outputs remain statistical estimates from a small benchmark dataset
- A "72% probability" means the model, trained on 303 patients, assigns that score — it does NOT mean the patient has a 72% chance of having heart disease
- SHAP values describe how features influenced **the model's mathematical output**, not how they caused or prevented a physical disease process

## What the System WILL NOT DO

- Diagnose any individual with heart disease
- Prescribe, recommend, or adjust medications
- Provide dosage information for any drug
- Tell a patient they are "safe" or "at risk" in clinical terms
- Generate emergency medical advice
- Replace laboratory tests, imaging, or clinical examination
- Claim that SHAP feature importance equals medical causation
